\section*{Abstract}
\label{sec:abstract}
Ho Chi Minh City's rapid urbanization presents critical challenges to its transportation infrastructure, characterized by a highly fragmented and congested mobility network. Commuters and residents are forced to manually synchronize the disparate rhythms of public bus schedules, and the overwhelming crescendo of private vehicles through different app. This lack of integration results in significant operational inefficiencies, including suboptimal routing, and chronic exposure to severe traffic congestion, which in turn impacts urban quality of life.

We propose the development of an integrated "HCMC Transportation" web platform, builed to unify these transit modalities. This system will provide users with a single point of access for route optimization, aggregating data from public bus systems, and predictive traffic models.

This document outlines a Proof of Concept (PoC). The PoC is designed to validate the core technical feasibility of data integration, user adoption hypotheses, and the platform's value proposition for safety and efficiency against rigorous, predefined performance metrics specific to the HCMC environment. Following a successful PoC, we project an agile 3-month, four-phase production schedule for full-scale deployment.
